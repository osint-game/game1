## OSINT practice game


Practise your skills using **Open Source Intelligence**.

Follow the clues, analyze the available information, and piece everything together using publicly accessible sources only.

The start and the finish come full circle here.
<br>
### Answer the questions using this form: 
https://form.typeform.com/to/NWIIeUlX

<br>

### The questions:

1. What is the website of the blog?
2. One post was published on a “day of ones”. What is the exact date?
3. Another platform is referenced in the blog. What is the username there?
4. This person is on a vacation. Which city was visited first?
5. Around Christmas this person visited a beautiful lake. Why is it colored the shade of a flamingo?
6. During a flight home, a pond can be seen from above. What is the name of this pond?
7. What animal is the person’s pet?
8. What is the person’s profession?
9. What is this person’s home city?
10. What could be printed as the final result?
